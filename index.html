01. WATCHING?

Powder was a scared little girl who hid in the shadows, waiting for someone to save her. Jinx doesn't wait. Jinx brings the fire. Can you feel the heat yet? Every time I blink, I see the lights of Piltover screaming to be put out. It's like a song that never stops, a high-pitched ringing in my ears that only quietens when something goes BOOM.

02. DEAREST SISTER

Violet. The name tastes like iron and regret. You told me I was a jinx. You told me to stay away. And then you left. You went to the topside and traded your rags for a badge. Does she think she can punch her way back into my heart? You can't fix what you shattered, Vi! I waited for years. I counted every second in the dark.

03. FATHER

Silco saw me. Not the mistake, but the masterpiece. He found a girl drowning in her own tears and told her she was perfect. He gave me a workshop, he gave me a purpose, and he gave me the world. He understood that to survive in Zaun, you have to stop caring about being "good." We'll show them all!

04. LITTLE MAN

Ekko thinks he’s so smart with his little clocks. We used to be friends, back when the world was just a playground of scrap metal. Now he looks at me with those sad, judgmental eyes. He wants to rewind time? Fine! Let him try. He’s stuck in a loop while I’m moving forward into the beautiful chaos.

05. MY BABIES

Meet Fishbones. He’s a bit of a pessimist, always talking about total annihilation. And Pow-Pow? She just likes to talk fast—very, very fast. They are the only ones who never lie to me. They don't leave, and they don't change their minds. When I hold them, the voices finally start to harmonize.

06. THE CHORUS

Can you hear them? The whispers? Mylo says I’m clumsy. Claggor just stares. They’re always there, sitting on my shoulders, watching me work. Sometimes I try to drown them out, but they just get louder. They keep me sharp. They keep me angry. Without the ghosts, I’d just be a girl.

07. EYES UP

The Shimmer is a funny thing. It feels like lightning running through my blood, turning my eyes into glowing embers. It saved my life, they say. But it also took the rest of what was left of Powder. Now, when I move, the world blurs into streaks of violet and neon. I feel stronger and much more... unstable.

08. THE THRONE

I made a seat for myself. Right at the top of the junk heap. From here, I can see the sparkling towers of the Council. They think they're safe. They think they're better than us. But a throne is just a chair until you decide to sit in it. And I'm not just sitting—I'm waiting for the moment to burn it down.

09. JUST KIDDING

Did I say I was going to blow everything up? I mean, probably. But look at this face! Does this look like the face of a cold-blooded criminal? Okay, don't answer that. Sometimes you just have to laugh at the voices. If you don't laugh, they win. And Jinx doesn't like losing. Let's have a tea party!

10. BYE BYE

This is it. The finale they’ll never forget. A shower of sparks and a sky turned pink. It’s time to stop hiding. It’s time to start the chaos. The fuse is lit, the rocket is aimed, and I’ve got a front-row seat. Don't blink, or you'll miss the best part of the show. Boom.
